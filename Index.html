Pre reading

• What do you know about polar habitats?
• Vocabulary:
• Antarctica - the coldest continent on Earth
• burrow - a tunnel or hole in the ground
• coast - the land beside an ocean
• habitat - the place where animals, plants, or people live
• krill - tiny animals that float in the ocean
• regions - -large areas of land or water

I Do

• Read as far as you can
• Think aloud: What do I remember
• Write 3-5 complete sentences with capitals and periods about polar animals, plants and places. • Draw a picture to match your writing.
